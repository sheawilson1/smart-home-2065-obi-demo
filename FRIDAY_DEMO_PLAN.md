# Obi Friday Demo Plan

## The Angle

Obi is not a generic smart home dashboard. It is a projected household interface that recognises who is present, changes its tone for them, and turns ordinary home surfaces into calm, contextual displays.

## Live Demo Arc

1. Start asleep on the marble surface.
2. Press `A` to stage Obi as Amara.
3. Use `Right Arrow` or `Space` to advance each voice beat.
4. Let the garden portal open and briefly drag the live view.
5. Press `O` once to show that the physical surface context can change.
6. Press `K` to switch to Kofi, then step through Kofi's beats.

## Presenter Script

Opening:

> This is Obi, an AI-powered smart home interface designed for the home of 2065. I wanted it to feel less like a phone screen on a wall and more like the house itself quietly becoming responsive.

Wake:

> When Amara enters the kitchen, Obi wakes on the surface and brings forward only what matters: time, a household update, and the live garden state.

Portal:

> The garden view is the key speculative moment. Rather than opening an app, the countertop becomes a window into another part of the home ecosystem.

Personalisation:

> Switching to Kofi changes the density, tone, and content. The point is not just voice control, but a home that understands different rhythms in the same shared space.

Close:

> For the final version, I would replace the browser voice with rendered voice clips and use a Remotion edit to show the ideal interaction flow without relying on live timing.

## Remotion Shot List

1. 0-4s: Sleeping marble surface, faint Obi glow.
2. 4-10s: Amara wake, cards resolve into place.
3. 10-18s: Garden health card opens the live portal.
4. 18-25s: Slow drag across the garden panorama.
5. 25-31s: Object/surface context changes.
6. 31-40s: Kofi mode switch with denser studio layout.
7. 40-45s: End on the Obi mark/status and quiet surface.
